Home / Our Story

Leather is patient. So are we.

Fabulus began at a single workbench with one belief: that a thing made by hand, to fit one person, is worth more than a thousand made for no one.

How it started

From one bench to a workshop

What began as evenings spent resoling neighbours' shoes grew, pair by pair, into a full atelier. We taught ourselves the old way — cutting patterns by hand, lasting each shoe, welting soles that can be repaired for a lifetime rather than thrown away.

Today the same hands make dress shoes, weave sandals and cut belts for customers across the country. What hasn't changed is the promise stitched into every piece: leave the rest to us.

What we stand for

Craft over quantity

Real materials

Only full-grain, vegetable-tanned leather — the kind that ages beautifully instead of peeling away.

A perfect fit

We measure, last and adjust until the shoe disappears on your foot. Fit is the whole point.

Built to be repaired

Welted construction means a worn sole is a repair, not a landfill. Our work is meant to outlast trends.
