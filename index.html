Craftsmanship

Built By
Hand, In
Port Harcourt

Every Fabulus Sizes pair starts as a hide, a last, and a sketch. We cut, stitch and finish each shoe in-house — no shortcuts, no factory runs — so the leather, the fit and the finish are exactly as promised.

We work across shoes, mules, sandals and slippers, and we stand behind what we make: our repair bench keeps old favourites walking for years longer.
