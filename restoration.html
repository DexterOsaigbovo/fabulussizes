Resuscitating Leather

Good leather rarely dies — it is simply neglected. We clean, repair, recolour and protect the leather you already own, restoring the beauty, durability and value of every piece that passes through our workshop.

Our commitment

Craftsmanship, not cover-up

Restoration is not a coat of paint over a problem. It is diagnosis, repair and refinishing — done in the right order, with the right materials, by hands that make leather goods from scratch every day.

Because we are shoemakers first, we understand how a piece is constructed before we take it apart. That is what separates a genuine restoration from a temporary cosmetic fix.

  • Honest assessment first — we tell you plainly whether a piece is worth restoring before you spend anything
  • Colour-matched finishes — dyes and pigments blended to your item, not the nearest tin on the shelf
  • Structure before cosmetics — we fix what holds the piece together before we make it beautiful
  • Protected on return — every item leaves conditioned and sealed against the next few years of wear

The process

How a restoration runs

Every item follows the same four stages, whether it is a pair of loafers or a three-seat sofa.

Assess & quote

We inspect the leather, identify the damage and its cause, and give you a clear scope and price before any work starts.

Clean & strip

Years of dirt, body oil, silicone polish and failed previous finishes are removed so we are working on the leather itself.

Repair & rebuild

Tears are backed and filled, seams re-stitched, panels and structure rebuilt. Nothing cosmetic happens until the piece is sound.

Finish & protect

Colour is matched and applied in thin layers, then sealed, conditioned and buffed to the sheen the piece originally had.

Conditions we treat

If it is leather, it can usually be saved

These are the problems we see most often. If yours is not listed, send a photo and we will tell you honestly what can be done.

Cracking & dryness

Leather starved of moisture splits along its flex points. We rehydrate the hide, fill the cracks and restore flexibility.

Fading & sun damage

UV bleaches colour unevenly, especially on car interiors and window-side furniture. We recolour the full panel for a consistent tone.

Scuffs & scratches

Surface abrasion on toes, corners and bolsters is levelled, re-pigmented and blended into the surrounding finish.

Tears & seam failure

Splits and burst stitching are backed with support material, filled and re-sewn so the repair carries load again.

Water & mould damage

Water staining, tide marks and mould growth are treated and neutralised, then the affected area is re-finished and sealed.

Dye transfer & stains

Denim transfer onto light leather, ink and grease marks are lifted where possible, then colour-corrected where they are not.

Aftercare

Keeping it that way

A restoration should begin a long second life, not become a treatment you repeat every year. Every piece is returned with care guidance specific to its leather and how you use it.

  • Condition lightly, often — a light feed every three to six months beats a heavy one once a year
  • Keep it out of direct sun — parked cars and window-side furniture fade fastest
  • Blot spills immediately — never rub, and never dry leather with direct heat
  • Rotate and rest footwear — a day off between wears lets linings dry and doubles their life
  • Store with structure — shoe trees for footwear, stuffing for bags, dust bags rather than plastic
