Cobbler services

Give your favourites a second life

A worn sole or a scuffed toe doesn't mean the end. Our cobblers repair, resole and restore shoes of any make — not only ours.

  • Resoling & heel repair — new leather or rubber soles, rebuilt heels
  • Stitch & seam repair — re-welting and blown-out seams
  • Recolour & refinish — deep clean, dye and hand-polish
  • Stretch & adjust — width and fit corrections
